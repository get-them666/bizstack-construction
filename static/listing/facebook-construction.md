weakest of the three for getting a homeowner to call — it is most useful for staying
visible to people who already follow you, and for the neighbours who can see your truck
on their street.

## 8. The honest caveat

Facebook pages for contractors mostly reach people who already know you or who follow
local groups. It is not where a cold homeowner finds you — Google is. Treat this as
cheap upkeep, not as your growth engine, and do not check it daily. If the page needs
daily posting to be worth anything, it isn't worth your 2 hours of sleep.
