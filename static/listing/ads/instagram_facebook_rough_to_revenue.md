# Ad Concept 1 — "Rough to Revenue" (Meta: Facebook & Instagram)

**Objective:** Drive investor / Airbnb host leads to the free walkthrough form.
**Audience:** Local real-estate investors, flippers, Airbnb/Vrbo hosts, 25–55,
within ~45 miles of Chesapeake / Williamsburg, VA and Elizabeth City / Currituck, NC.
**Placements:** Feed, Stories, Reels.
**CTA:** Book Free Walkthrough → construction.bizstackperks.com/quote

---

### Visual idea
Split-screen or carousel: neglected, outdated house on the left → beautifully
staged, modern Airbnb interior on the right. Optionally a 3-frame carousel:
"before" → "during (crew at work)" → "after (staged listing photo)."

---

### Primary text
Stop juggling four different contractors and endless turnover headaches. 🛠️✨

BuildStack Construction takes your investment property down to the studs and
brings it back to move-in ready with one crew, one fixed price, and zero stress.

Even better? Our in-house Broom Service division takes over the moment
construction wraps — handling your Airbnb cleanings, linens, and guest
operations seamlessly. We turn your rough property into passive revenue.

One company. One contract. From rough to revenue.

---

### Headline
Buy it. Fix it. List it. We do the rest.

---

### Description (link)
Licensed general contractor serving Williamsburg–Hampton Roads, VA and
Currituck County & Elizabeth City, NC. Free on-site walkthrough, written fixed
price.

---

### Lead-qualification add-on (instant quote)
When they click through, the `/instant-quote` tool asks for the address, pulls
square footage & build year from public records, and answers:

- Cosmetic make-ready: sq ft × $25–$45
- Full rehab: sq ft × $75–$120 (depending on age)

Then asks: "Is this a fix-and-flip, a long-term rental, or an Airbnb?" — so you
know exactly how to pitch when you call.
