people who are already ready to hire. Yelp and Facebook are cheap extras after this
is live and verified.

verification codes and review replies go there.

## 7. Verification — the part that stalls everyone

Google will post a card or send a code. **This is the step you have to do yourself** —
it goes to your phone or your mail, and I can't accept anything on your behalf.

- **Search** → a Google search card
- **Video** → record your storefront or, since you're service-area, walk your camera

If you have **no** job currently underway, this is the one thing that genuinely blocks
the profile. Everything else here you can finish tonight.

## 8. After it's verified — the two things that actually drive calls

1. **Ask every past customer for a review.** Text or call them yourself. A review from
   a real person who lived in the house is worth more than anything in this document.
   Your phone already sends the bot out; ask manually for this one.
2. **Post weekly.** One photo of real work, one sentence. A profile with a post from
   this week outranks an identical dormant profile.

## 9. Do not do these

- **Don't buy reviews.** Google suspends for it, and it reads worse than no reviews.
- **Don't add keywords to the business name.** Suspension risk.
- **Don't create a second profile** for Broom Service on the same address and phone.
  That is a duplicate-location suspension. Broom Service gets its own profile with its
  own category (Cleaning service) later.
- **Don't list the home address.** Service-area business, hidden address.
