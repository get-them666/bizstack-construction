## Why this order

Google Business Profile is the only one of the three where someone actively searching
"contractor near me" or "kitchen remodel Virginia Beach" finds you. Facebook mostly
reaches people who already follow you. Yelp is a genuine second source of the same
searches as Google.

If you only have one evening, do Google and stop.

## The one step you cannot skip

Google verification. It is a postcard or a video of a real job site, and it goes to you.
Everything in these documents is copy-paste; that step is the only one that physically
requires you, and I can't take it on your behalf.

**If you don't have an active job site to film** — and two workers currently have no
work — that's the thing to sort out. Ask a current or recent customer whether you can
film their finished project. That is a normal thing to ask and it gets you both the
verification video and a review from the same person.

## The mistake to avoid

**Do not list your home address.** `701 Dana Dr` is the return address on the letterhead
so USPS can route a bounced letter back — it is not a business location. Choose
"service-area business" and hide the address. Also don't create a Broom Service profile
on the same phone and address later; that's a duplicate-location suspension. Give
Broom its own profile with its own cleaning category.
