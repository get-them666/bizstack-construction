## 3. Photos

Yelp accepts up to 30. Add the same 10 you upload to Google — same work, two more
places it shows up. `1.png` through `4.png` in `static/listing/` are ready to go.

## 4. Requesting reviews

Yelp's review flow is worse than Google's, so don't wait on it. Use the same list of
past customers you'd ask on Google. Real job descriptions beat "Great contractor!"

## 5. Notes

- **Free tier is genuinely fine.** Yelp's paid ads are expensive and untested here —
  don't spend money on them until the profile has reviews and is converting.
- **Never buy reviews.** Yelp is aggressive about this and it can shut the page.
- **Answer every review**, good or bad. One thoughtful reply to a 3-star is worth more
  to a future customer than another 5-star.
- Yelp shows reviews as "recommended" — they still work.
