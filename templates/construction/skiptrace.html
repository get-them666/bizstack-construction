Owner Lookup

Look up the owner of record for an address from the public assessor record. This is the same data behind the "Who owns this property?" button on each permit, and it costs nothing on an address you have checked before.

About 40% of permit records have no ZIP. Leave it blank — the lookup still works, and the result is flagged as a city-level match.
