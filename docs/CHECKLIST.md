## SWaM certification pre-stage (also serves the loan package)

The SBSD document list overlaps most of the items above, so gather once and use twice.
Statuses below are SBSD's requirement, **not** a claim about what you have on hand.

### Owner identity & control
| Item | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **VA DMV license** (owner, 51%+) | ☐ | SBSD requires this **AND** one item below |
| Passport **or** birth certificate **or** permanent resident card **or** naturalization cert | ☐ | U.S. citizenship or legal residency is required for **Small** cert regardless of other designations |
| Detailed chronological **resume** — all ownership/employment with dates | ☐ | Cheap to write, and doubles as loan-package proof of 25 yrs in the trade |

### Financials — **you can skip these, you're a new business**
| Item | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| ~~Complete federal business return, most recent year~~ | **N/A** | Only for established entities |
| ~~First page of the two prior years~~ | **N/A** | Skip — no 3-year history exists yet |
| ~~Form 941, last four quarters~~ | **N/A** | Established entities only |
| *If sole prop / Sch. C:* full personal return | **N/A** | Established entities only |
| **No-employees owner letter** (signed + dated) | ☐ | ✅ **Drafted** — `docs/NO-EMPLOYEE-OWNER-LETTER.html`. Print, fill the two bracketed fields (contractor license #, start date), sign, date. Closes the 941 question outright. |
| **Confirm the new-business lookback with SBSD** | ☐ | Call **(804) 786-6585**. Ask specifically: *"As a business in existence less than three years, do I substitute the EIN letter for the 3-year return requirement?"* Get the answer in your portal record |

> ✅ **The three-year receipts gap is not a blocker.** **§ 2.2-4310** sets the size test at
> $10M over "up to each of its last three fiscal years **or lesser time period if it has
> been in existence less than three years**." And SBSD's checklist requires 3 years of
> returns from *all applicants*, then adds a separate **"New Businesses"** section
> requiring **only proof of EIN** — which is exactly the carve-out for someone who has no
> 3-year history. You qualify on the size test by a factor of ~300×.
