# BuildStack Construction: no-cost marketing starter kit

This is an organic, $0 media-spend starter plan. Paid placements, boosted posts, provider subscriptions, postage, and API credits are not free and must be approved and budgeted separately. No ad is published by this repository.

## Positioning

Local residential/light-commercial contractor serving the configured service area. Lead with completed work, specific trades actually staffed, photos with permission, written scopes, clear estimates, and a simple request for a walkthrough. State licensing, insurance, availability, warranties, customer ratings, and savings only when verified and current. Do not claim guaranteed start dates, fixed prices from ballparks, or qualifications not confirmed for this entity.

## Organic campaign ideas (BuildStack only)

1. **Project proof** — one permitted job photo set, scope, city, challenge, and what the customer approved. Get written photo/testimonial permission first; remove personal details and exact addresses.
2. **Useful seasonal checklist** — roof, drainage, exterior, or winter-prep guidance tied to a real local need; CTA to the free walkthrough/estimate page.
3. **Trade explainer** — short “what changes the price?” post using real assumptions (size, access, materials, concealed conditions), not made-up prices.
4. **Referral partner introduction** — realtor/property-manager/inspector collaboration. Use an individual introduction, not a bulk-scraped address list.
5. **Bid capability sheet** — verified service area, trades, insurance/licensing details, references that have consented, and a direct estimating contact; keep it separate from consumer marketing.

Suggested no-cost distribution: business-owned social profiles, Google Business Profile posts if the listing is eligible, local business/community groups whose rules allow it, referral partners, and the website’s existing portfolio/quote pages. Respect each platform’s rules and do not automate unsolicited DMs or scraped contact harvesting.

## Ready-to-edit email drafts

### Requested estimate follow-up (send only to the person who requested the estimate)
**Subject:** Next step for your [project type] estimate

Hi [first name],

Thanks for asking BuildStack Construction about [project type] in [city]. The range on the website is an early planning estimate, not a final bid. A walkthrough lets us confirm the scope, access, material choices, and any conditions that could change the price.

Would [time option 1] or [time option 2] work? You can also reply with a better time or call/text [verified business number].

[Business name]
[Verified mailing address]

### Referral partner introduction (one-to-one, relevant business contact)
**Subject:** Local construction estimating contact for [area]

Hi [name],

I’m [sender] with [business name]. We’re available for [verified trades] in [service area]. If a client needs that kind of work, I’d be glad to explain our estimate and project process. No referral commitment is expected.

Would a short introduction be useful? [Website] · [verified phone]

[Business name and verified postal address]

For promotional mail, include the required truthful sender details and a working unsubscribe mechanism. Send only to people with documented marketing permission or a valid, reviewed basis; maintain and honor a suppression list. Do not add permit-only, inferred, scraped, or purchased contacts to marketing lists.
